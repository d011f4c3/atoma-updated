## foil-trio

Use case: product-mockup. Create an exceptional photorealistic studio concept image of THREE small flat silver foil sample sachets for a precise contemporary matcha brand, each sized as a compact 30 gram tea pouch. Composition: a cohesive fanned trio, central pouch frontmost vertical, rear left pouch turned about -15 degrees, rear right +12 degrees; all three complete and legible silhouettes, compact low sculpture, generous clear margins. Camera slightly above front, neutral long lens, NOT overhead. Heat sealed edges, realistic brushed aluminum micro-wrinkles, subtle shallow pouch volume, no zipper spout. Each pouch has one clean small blank ivory rectangular paper label on lower front, absolutely NO typography, logos, marks or green printing. Neutral cool silver with broad beautiful specular gradients, dark folds, sharp crafted physical details. Soft directional photographic lighting from upper left, delicate contact shadows. Transparent alpha background and no surface, backdrop, environment, props or tabletop. The entire trio centered and fully visible, 1024 square composition with about 12 percent clear margin. Luxury editorial still life, quiet clinical production-engineering character. Not lifestyle, not cosmetics, not cartoon, not CGI plastic. This is a packaging concept, not documentary product photography.

## open-set

Use case: product-mockup. Photorealistic design concept of an OPEN sample presentation carton for a clinical contemporary matcha brand. One thin elegant cool light-gray rigid paperboard rectangular box, with a hinged lid opened backwards at about 110 degrees. Viewed from elevated three-quarter perspective (camera45 degrees above). Exactly THREE compact silver foil tea sachets nestled side by side in a single row in the base, clearly visible, softly reflective aluminum and realistic fine wrinkles, each with a small blank ivory rectangular label, NO text or logos anywhere. The lid interior is plain cool gray. The box is compact not deep; the trio of sachets is the primary subject. Precise construction edges, slight shadow gaps between packets, restrained beautiful material depth. Neutral soft directional studio light from top-left, cool silver and pale-gray materials, no decorative colors. Entire object and lid fully visible, centered with 12 percent margin, 1024 square composition. Transparent alpha background, no table, background, props, ribbons, tissue, plants, humans or printing. Art-directed luxury product still life; subtly tactile real photograph, never plastic CGI. This is unconfirmed packaging concept art.

## powder-landscape

Use case: product-mockup. Create a sculptural photorealistic macro still life of THREE separate small low mounds of fine matcha tea powder, arranged into one beautiful low horizontal landscape. They are natural gently irregular mounds, NOT brush swatches, NOT vertical smears, NOT perfect cones, NOT circular disks. Left mound cool vivid fresh matcha green, middle deeper muted grass green, right slightly olive tea green. Fine velvety grains with delicate irregular ridges, softly collapsed sides and a few scattered granules close to each base. Camera from a very low front three-quarter angle approximately20 degrees above the base, long lens. The center mound just behind the other two, each of the three clearly distinguishable with a little separation. Deliberate asymmetry and small variations in peak height, all objects fully visible; broad low landscape composition occupying central70 percent of square frame. Large empty transparent margin above and below, authentic alpha transparency, no solid floor/background, no bowl, tray, spoon, utensil, tea leaves, plants, typography, scientific marks or other objects. Neutral studio lighting from upper left makes texture exquisite, soft physically plausible contact shadows. Tactile photographic realism, quiet precise production-engineering brand mood, not fantasy hills or mountains. This is an illustrative material composition, not documentary exact tea imagery.
