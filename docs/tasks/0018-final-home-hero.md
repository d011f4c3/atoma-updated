## Scope

Refine the preferred homepage layout at `/` and `/light`. Use Concept 05's
actual filled, indexed Matcha/About button styling in the compact right-hand
navigation. Preserve the strong split composition, original tray and typography,
and scoped curved field. Keep linework limited to layout separation and the
product's visual field; remove surplus registration marks and the moving node.
Make the appearance choice quiet text links with a selected underline and usable
focus/touch targets. Keep black/blue palettes, subtle text resolves, and one
viewport fit. No surface inspection: the owner's clarified goal is real product
selection, with modern laboratory character and no decorative instrumentation.

Both original client documents were reread in full during this task. The design
follows their emphasis on intrigue, product entry, neutral thin typography, and
progressive information rather than explaining origin or people on arrival.

The owner also requests a considered path into product listings and the rest of
the site. Define this concretely in a brief-led UX document: product selection
before origin/people, useful verified catalog information, transition behavior,
and navigation. The implementation remains the hero and its real About dialog;
no fabricated product listing, new commerce connection, or placeholder shopping
action. Explore matcha remains the proposed catalog entrance, unlinked until the
catalog slice. Preserve all numbered comparison concepts and shared components.
