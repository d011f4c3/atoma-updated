visual language: selection for specific applications, the company behind ATOMA,
and relationships with places and communities. Keep the existing About dialog
useful and provide a clear route to deeper reading without disrupting product
selection. Confirm the exact legal/company disclosure before publication.

the actual activity, place, date, source and publication permission. Add a story
only when evidence exists; future intentions must be labelled as such.
Community involvement must never imply the origin of every product. Preserve
the current private-grower policy until specific identity publication is approved.

- No empty promotional story grid, new CMS or operations-management feature.
- Theme, metadata, navigation, focus and return to product selection work.

Run relevant route/navigation tests, browser checks in both themes,
`pnpm validate` and scoped diff review. Production publication is separate.
