# Task 0287 — Community harvest and technology stories

Date: 2026-10-23
Status: Complete

The owner rejected the inflated Community headings and camera narration, then
clarified that short photographic descriptions were not the requested outcome.
The final direction is two complete stories: farmers harvesting tea for matcha,
and powersuits supporting aging bodies alongside drones for field management.
This supersedes the initial caption-only rewrite.

The harvest story follows farmers guiding the harvester, collecting and moving
bags of leaves, then explains how shade-grown tea becomes tencha and matcha.
The technology story gives separate sections to physical support during harvest
and aerial inspection followed by checking the fields on foot. Owner-supplied
subjects establish the Wazuka grower and equipment context. No interviews,
identities, equipment specifications, measured results or product provenance
are invented.

Shared chapter paragraphs now appear in both the standalone pages and popup
readers. About cards, related-story titles and metadata share the new titles and
introductions. Japanese and both Chinese translations cover the complete prose.
Photography, composite labeling and global navigation controls are retained.

General factual context is supported by:

- [MAFF: types of green tea](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/heya/sodan/1404/01.html):
  shading, steaming and drying without rolling, tencha, then milling.
- [NARO: Kyoto tea-farm technology demonstration](https://www.naro.go.jp/smart-nogyo/r2/files/r2_cha-F01.pdf):
  drone inspection of field condition, work progress and weather damage, with
  follow-up inspection on foot. This is general context, not a claim about the
  photographed farm's equipment or results.
- [MAFF: agricultural assist suits](https://www.affrc.maff.go.jp/docs/project/seika/2020/r2_seikashu_08.html):
  physical assistance for agricultural work, including older farmers.

Scoped formatting, ESLint and TypeScript passed, as did all seven existing
localization tests and coverage of the new prose in all three translated
languages. Both article pages render all four body paragraphs. Desktop harvest
and mobile technology views were checked for overflow and visually reviewed.
No broad suite or deployment, per the owner's request.
