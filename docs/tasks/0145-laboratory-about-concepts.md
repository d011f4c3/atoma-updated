2. **Working method:** staged matcha in a metal tray with restrained pointer

cursor and is disabled for reduced motion. All text remains readable without

photography to come. No generated people, private identities, funding results,
product origin implications or invented technical claims. Both concepts preserve
company and provenance boundaries, the shared header/footer, cart/theme state and
archived studies. No deployment or new dependencies.
