# Task 0113 — Evaluate a common pouch and product-specific labels

Date: 2026-10-06
Status: Planned — supplier costs and label requirements needed
Source: [Feedback §8](../briefs/2026-10-06-client-feedback.md)

## Outcome and scope

Compare the present information-heavy pouch with a common ATOMA pouch carrying
changeable product and batch labels. Preserve the accepted silver-packaging
appearance while reducing the cost of updating information.

Prepare a content allocation and one reviewable mockup: common pouch for stable
brand elements; product label for name, corrected code, intended use and net
weight; back/batch label for required information, storage and best-before; a
stable website/QR destination for tasting, preparation, origins and stories.
Verify required physical-label content for the agreed launch destinations with
the appropriate current primary guidance and business reviewer. A QR code must
not be treated as a replacement for mandatory on-pack information.

Obtain printing method, label compatibility, minimum order quantity, setup and
unit costs, lead time and durability constraints. Compare total costs for a
small launch run and later product updates. No printing order, new SKU, pouch
specification, compliance certification or storefront artwork replacement is
approved in advance.

## Acceptance and checks

The proposal shows which information can change independently, how products
remain distinguishable, where future batch details are applied, and what each
option costs. Verify physical-size legibility, label contrast, QR readability
and stable destinations before production artwork. Any website changes receive
their own bounded brief, relevant browser checks and `pnpm validate`.
