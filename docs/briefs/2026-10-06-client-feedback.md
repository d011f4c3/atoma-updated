The client approves the current silver packaging, powder texture, quiet palette
and fine-line design. The next work should help customers understand the range,
try samples and continue ordering while retaining that presentation.

1. **Samples and repeat ordering.** One set contains 30 g each of Ceremonial,
   Barista and Culinary, with entry points on home, Shop and product pages.
   Explain applications, quantities, price, destination shipping and dispatch
   estimate. Support progression to 1 kg and repeat orders; assess incentives,
   reordering, guidance and subscriptions against actual costs and demand.
   JPY 4,800 and a JPY 1,000 equivalent credit are reference ideas, not approved
   prices or terms. Expiry and subscription frequencies remain flexible.
2. **Destination-based selling.** Country may initially be suggested by IP, with
   a manual delivery-country selector. Final prices, currency and conditions
   follow actual destination. Shipping reflects destination, quantity and
   sample/single/multiple-pouch packaging. Show the total before payment and
   distinguish preparation before dispatch from carrier transit.
3. **Confirmed product facts.** Publish the ingredients, storage, shelf life,
   origins and other details already confirmed by Made in Kyoto, using Shopify
   information or a simple maintainable existing mechanism. Those actual values
   are not included in this feedback. Pouch best-before and internal lot records
   remain separate; no facts may be inferred to fill the gaps.
4. **Product codes.** Ceremonial is `WZKA-00`, Barista `UJI-00`, Culinary `UJI-01`.
   Numbers identify products within a series; they do not rank quality. The
   owner explicitly requires backend alignment as well as UI labels.
5. **Origins.** Replace the claim that all three products are Wazuka-grown.
   Barista and Culinary are associated with the UJI tea designation. Ceremonial
   retains Wazuka. Distinguish designation, actual growing location and
   processing location. Kyoto is the prefecture; Uji City and Wazuka Town are
   separate municipalities. Keep records, copy, photos and related products easy
   to extend without an origin-management system.
6. **About.** Add a simple About ATOMA page for application-led selection, the
   operating company and relationships with places and communities. Allow later
   evidenced activity stories/photos. Separate completed work from intentions
   and community involvement from product provenance.
7. **Readability.** Keep mono/uppercase for short labels and codes. Use clearer
   sentence-case body copy, comfortable size/contrast and shorter instructions
   for Overview, Application & Preparation, Product Record/Details and Origins.
   Keep specifications as the starting point. Trial use comparisons and imagery
   only when helpful; preserve the design and mobile usability.
8. **Packaging.** Explore a common branded pouch with a product label for name,
   code, intended use and net weight; a back/batch label for required information,
   storage and best-before; and a website/QR destination for deeper detail.
   Printing method, minimum runs, unit costs and required content determine the
   final proposal. No packaging purchase or compliance claim is approved here.
